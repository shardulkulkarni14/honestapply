# Commercial licensing & managed hosting

**honestapply is free and open source under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).** For most people —
individuals running it on their own machine, or teams self-hosting it internally — the
AGPL is all you need, at no cost.

This page is for the cases where the AGPL doesn't fit, and for teams who would rather
not run it themselves at all.

## When you need a commercial license

The AGPL-3.0 is strong copyleft. In particular, if you **modify honestapply and make it
available to others over a network** (for example, offering it as a hosted service), the
AGPL requires you to release your modified source under the AGPL as well.

You likely want a **commercial license** — which removes the AGPL's copyleft obligations
— if you want to:

- **Embed** honestapply (or parts of it) inside a **closed-source product** you ship or
  sell.
- **Offer a hosted or managed service** based on a modified honestapply **without**
  releasing your modifications.
- Use it inside an organization whose policy **prohibits AGPL-licensed software**.

Under a commercial license you get the same code under proprietary terms, without the
copyleft requirements. Because the maintainer holds the copyright (and all contributors
sign the [CLA](CLA.md)), this dual-licensing is available directly from the source.

## Managed hosting (fully hosted — you don't run anything)

If you just want the outcome without operating the pipeline yourself, a **managed
edition** is on the roadmap: we host and run it, you use it. As a *user* of a hosted
service you take on **no** AGPL distribution obligations — the copyleft only applies to
those who distribute or network-serve the software, not to end users.

If you'd like early access to managed hosting, say so when you get in touch.
