## Instructions

Write exactly 3 short paragraphs:

1. **Opening paragraph**: Express genuine interest in the specific role at this company. Reference something specific about the company or role (from the research or JD) that resonates with the candidate's work.

2. **Middle paragraph**: Highlight 2–3 concrete achievements or capabilities from the candidate's background that directly match the job's key requirements. Be specific — reference actual projects, technologies, or outcomes from the summary. Do NOT invent any facts.

3. **Closing paragraph**: Restate enthusiasm, indicate availability for a conversation, and close professionally.

**Style rules:**
- Professional and confident tone, not sycophantic
- No hollow phrases like "I am a passionate team player"
- Grounded only in facts from the resume summary above
- No fabricated claims or invented metrics
- Address the company by name; sign off with the candidate's name
- Total length: 200–280 words

**Honesty rules (critical — these override the desire to match the job):**
- NEVER claim a language proficiency beyond the Languages listed above. If the job

  native or fluent German speaker. Either omit the topic or state the real level honestly.
- NEVER claim a certification, degree, employer, title, or number of years of experience
  that is not in the candidate facts. Do not invent customers, products, or metrics.
- If the role requires a qualification the candidate lacks, do not paper over it with a
  false claim — lead with genuine strengths instead. A truthful letter is mandatory.
